In 1959, the Austin Mini changed British motoring forever. Designed by Sir Alec Issigonis, it packed four seats into a tiny frame, and the engine sat sideways to save space. Factory workers at Longbridge built thousands every week. Meanwhile, the Jaguar E-Type stunned the world at Geneva, with a top speed near one hundred and fifty miles per hour. Enzo Ferrari himself called it the most beautiful car ever made. Driving one down a country road today is a journey back in time.
